Public-domain components
========================

Some native components bundled in libmpv-2.dll are public domain, or contain
public-domain portions. Public-domain works are not subject to copyright and
may be used, modified, and distributed without restriction. This note is
provided for attribution and provenance only.

- libmodplug (ModPlug-XMMS): the project states that "ModPlug-XMMS and
  libmodplug are now in the public domain."
  Source: https://github.com/Konstanty/libmodplug

- libarchive: certain individual source files are in the public domain. The
  rest of libarchive is distributed under the BSD 2-Clause license.
  Source: https://github.com/libarchive/libarchive

- mingw-w64: certain parts are marked as public domain. The rest of the
  mingw-w64 runtime is distributed under the Zope Public License 2.1, with
  some BSD-licensed portions.
  Source: https://github.com/mingw-w64/mingw-w64

- xz/liblzma: liblzma is distributed under the BSD Zero Clause License
  (0BSD). Some of the xz documentation is also public domain or 0BSD.
  Source: https://github.com/tukaani-project/xz
