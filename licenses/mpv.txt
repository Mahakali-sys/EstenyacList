The binary is libmpv-2.dll from the published zhongfly/mpv-winbuild
2026-10-26-baa3e11d42 release archive. Its SHA-256 matches that release
artifact, its build timestamp is 2026-10-26 12:32:30, and it reports FFmpeg
N-128097-g46f23be8b. The native component attribution uses

1f8d2e76255abc48f0456d9be99b4cab9c6566e6, the newest revision before the
build timestamp. The exact revision of each moving-branch dependency is
