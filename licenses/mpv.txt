Provenance note
---------------

The binary reports mpv v0.41.0-1308-gbaa3e11d4, a 2026-10-25 build, and
identifies as a zhongfly/mpv-winbuild build. Its SHA-256 does not match the
published zhongfly 2026-10-25 or 2026-10-26 archives. The native component
attribution uses the newest shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake revision before the
binary's build timestamp. The exact revision of each moving-branch dependency
is therefore inferred from the build date and the pinned build scripts; the
upstream project links in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md identify the source.
